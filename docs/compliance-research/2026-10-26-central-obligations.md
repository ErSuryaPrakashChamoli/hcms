## 1. Status of the four Labour Codes (as of 2026-10-26)

### 1.1 Commencement of the codes

All four Codes came into force on **21 November 2025**. The PIB release of 21 Nov 2025 says the Codes are "being made effective from 21st November 2025, rationalising 29 existing labour laws" (confirmed-official, PIB PRID 2192463).

| Code | Commencement notification (21 Nov 2025) | Provisions left out |
|---|---|---|
| Code on Wages, 2019 | S.O. 5322(E) | s.42(1)–(3) (Advisory Board) and s.67(2)(s),(t). Everything else in force, including Chapter IV (Bonus). |
| Industrial Relations Code, 2020 | S.O. 5320(E) | None; whole Code in force. |
| Code on Social Security, 2020 | S.O. 5319(E) | Parts of s.15(3) onward, s.16 other than (1)(c), s.164(1) item 3 and s.164(2)(b). s.142 (Aadhaar) has been in force since 3 May 2021 (S.O. 1730(E)). |
| OSH & Working Conditions Code, 2020 | S.O. 5321(E) | None; whole Code in force. |

Confidence for the S.O. numbers and exclusions: **secondary** (TaxGuru summary of the notifications). BDO's alert is consistent: Wages and Social Security Codes partly notified, IR and OSH Codes fully notified. The commencement date itself is confirmed-official.

**Title correction (Wages rules).** Taxmann reports a corrigendum, G.S.R. 629(E) dated 15 Jul 2026, that renames them the "**Wages (Central) Rules, 2026**" (secondary). It is corroborated by the SS Rules Gazette text of 8 May 2026: rule 53 cross-refers to "Form I/IV/IX/V of the **Wages (Central) Rules, 2026**" (confirmed-official). Use "Wages (Central) Rules, 2026" in labels, with "Code on Wages (Central) Rules, 2026" as an alias.

### 1.3 Transition position

**PIB, 21 Nov 2025 (confirmed-official):** "During transition, the relevant provisions of the existing labour Acts and their respective rules, regulations, notifications, standards, schemes, etc. will continue to remain in force."

**Central sphere.** On 8 May 2026 the central rules replaced the old central rules for establishments where the Central Government is the "appropriate Government". Under Code on Wages s.2(d), that means central-government establishments, railways, mines, oil fields, major ports, air transport, telecom, banking and insurance companies, CPSUs and their subsidiaries, and contractors of these. Each supersession clause carries "except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession".

**Ease of Compliance Rules, 2017 (Forms A–E).** These were notified by G.S.R. 154(E) on 21 Feb 2017 (confirmed-official). They are now **fully superseded for the central sphere**. The Wages rules supersede them for the MW, PW and ER Acts. The OSH rules supersede them for the BOCW, CLRA, ISMW, Mines, SPE and WJ Acts. Together these cover all nine Acts the 2017 rules applied to.

**State sphere.** Most private-sector shops, offices and IT/ITES companies are state-sphere for wages, OSH, gratuity and maternity. For them, state rules under the Codes apply. Until a state notifies final rules, the old state rules continue under the PIB transition statement. Which states have final rules is for the state-research agent.

**Effect on the product.**
- Central forms are the reference layout. Many state drafts mirror them, but this is not confirmed here.
- Keep the 2017 Forms A–E as a legacy template, selectable for periods before 8 May 2026 and for states still on old rules.
- EPF and ESIC are run by central bodies (EPFO, ESIC) under schemes and regulations that continue. Their due dates did not change with the Codes.

**Registration update (deadline imminent).** OSH rule 3(6) requires establishments already registered under any other central labour law to "update the registration particulars in FORM-I" on the Shram Suvidha portal "within six months from the date on which rules come into force". That works out to about **8 Nov 2026** (computed from 8 May 2026). Any change in Form I particulars must be updated within 30 days (rule 3(8)). Confidence: confirmed-official; the date is computed.

## 2. Registers under the central rules (final)

### 2.1 Common rules for all registers

- **Electronic maintenance is allowed.** Wages Rules r.51(1) says "electronically or in physical form". SS Rules r.53(1)(b) says "electronically or otherwise". OSH Rules r.72(1)(iv) says the same, and the OSH forms note that the register "may also be maintained electronically capturing, inter-alia the following details". Signature columns are needed only for physical registers ("Required in case register is maintained physically").
- **Retention.**
  - Wages Rules r.51(4): "preserved for a period of five years after the date of last entry".
  - SS r.53(1)(e) and OSH r.72(1)(vii): "preserved in original for a period of five calendar years from the date of last entry".
  - OSH leave register (r.76(2)): 5 years, and "shall not be destroyed even after the expiry of that period unless it has been properly transferred to the new register".
- **Location.** Registers must be kept at the office, the nearest convenient building within the precincts, or within a 3 km radius (SS r.53(3), OSH r.72(4)).
- **Language.**
  - SS: "English and Hindi or the language understood by a majority".
  - OSH: "English or Hindi and in local language".
- **No duplicate registers.** OSH r.72(3) and the SS r.53(1)(a) proviso both say registers kept under the Wages rules (Forms I, IV, IX, V) are deemed to be kept under the OSH and SS rules too. In practice, one set of three registers plus the wage slip covers all three Codes. OSH Forms XIII, XIV, XV and XVI are content-identical twins of Wages Forms I, IX, IV and V.
- **Header block on every register.** Name of the establishment, name of the employer, name of the owner, PAN/TAN of the employer, and Registration Number. The forms state: "Labour Identification Number (LIN) shall be the Registration Number of the Establishment".

#### `wage_payment_timeliness`

- **Code on Wages s.17(1):**
  - Daily-paid: at the end of the shift.
  - Weekly-paid: on the last working day of the week.
  - Fortnightly-paid: before the end of the second day after the fortnight.
  - Monthly-paid: "before the expiry of the seventh day of the succeeding month".
- **s.17(2):** on removal, dismissal, retrenchment, resignation or closure, wages are due "within two working days".
- **Contract labour (OSH r.98(8)):** if the contractor does not pay within 7 days of the wage period, the principal employer must pay within 15 days and recover the amount from the contractor.
- **Report idea:** a late-payment and full-and-final exception report.
- **Confidence:** confirmed-official (statute text via advocatekhoj bare act; Gazette for the OSH rule).

- **Entitlement (secondary; MoLE FAQ 30 Dec 2025 as reported by SCC Online):**

## 4. Wage definition and the 50% rule

**Source.** Code on Wages s.2(y) (confirmed-official statute text). The Code on Social Security s.2(88) carries the same definition, so it drives PF, ESI-chapter, gratuity and maternity computations; that link is secondary.

**Included:** "all remuneration ... and includes (i) basic pay; (ii) dearness allowance; and (iii) retaining allowance".

**Excluded** (clauses (a)–(k)):
- (a) statutory bonus not forming part of the terms of employment
- (b) house accommodation, light, water, medical and similar amenities
- (c) employer contributions to pension or PF, and interest on them
- (d) conveyance allowance or travel concession
- (e) special expenses of the job
- (f) HRA
- (g) remuneration under an award or settlement
- (h) overtime allowance
- (i) commission
- (j) gratuity on termination
- (k) retrenchment compensation, retirement benefit or ex-gratia on termination

**The 50% proviso:** "if payments made by the employer to the employee under clauses (a) to (i) exceeds one-half ... of the all remuneration calculated under this clause, the amount which exceeds such one-half ... shall be deemed as remuneration and shall be accordingly added in wages". Gratuity (j) and retrenchment compensation (k) are outside the 50% pool. Two further points:
- For equal wages and payment of wages, items (d), (f), (g) and (h) count as wages anyway.
- Remuneration in kind up to 15% of total wages is deemed wages.

**MoLE FAQs, 30 Dec 2025** (secondary; reported by SCC Online):
- The excess over 50% "shall be added back to wages".
- Performance incentives, ESOPs, variable pay and reimbursements "shall not be part of the wages".
- Leave encashment "is not a part of allowances".
- The revision raises "the base for PF, gratuity, and bonus".
- Gratuity on the revised definition applies "w.e.f. 21-11-2025".

**Why it matters for computation:**
- **PF:** contributions are on "wages". If basic + DA is below 50% of total remuneration, the excess exclusions are added back, which raises PF wages. The rise is capped at the wage ceiling (₹25,000 from 17 Sep 2026) where the employer restricts contributions to the ceiling.
- **Gratuity:** there is no wage ceiling, only the ₹20 lakh cap on the amount. A higher deemed "wage" raises gratuity directly: last-drawn wage ÷ 26 × 15 × years. This is the largest cost effect, and it applies from 21 Nov 2025.
- **Bonus:** the base is "wages" under s.2(y), but it is capped at ₹7,000 or the minimum wage (§5), so the effect is limited.

**Product design:** compute and store a "Code wage" per employee per period: basic + DA + retaining allowance + add-back of excluded items over 50%. Show the add-back on the wage register and payslip computation.

## 5. Bonus (Code on Wages, Chapter IV, ss.26–41; in force 21 Nov 2025)

**Applicability** (s.41(2), confirmed-official): establishments where 20 or more persons are or were employed on any day of the accounting year. Exempt under s.41(1): LIC, RBI, departmental and government undertakings, universities and educational institutions, non-profit hospitals, and others; public sector only where s.40 applies.

**Eligibility** (s.26(1)): "drawing wages not exceeding such amount per mensem, as determined by notification" and "has put in at least thirty days work in an accounting year". The accounting year starts 1 April (s.2(a)).
- **₹21,000 per month**, by S.O. 4711(E) dated 25 Aug 2026, deemed effective 21 Nov 2025.
- Confidence: secondary (SCC Online, TeamLease RegTech, Pradeep's blog). Gazette not viewed.

**Calculation ceiling** (s.26(2)): if wages exceed the notified amount, bonus is computed as if the wage were "**₹7,000** or the minimum wage fixed by the appropriate Government, whichever is higher". This is S.O. 4710(E) of 25 Aug 2026, deemed effective 21 Nov 2025 (secondary).
- Commentary disputes whether these Central S.O.s bind state-sphere establishments; the argument is that the minimum wage of the appropriate (State) Government applies. Treat as open and **NOT CONFIRMED**.

**Rates and timing:**
- Minimum: 8⅓% of wages earned or ₹100, whichever is higher, payable whether or not there is allocable surplus (s.26(1)).
- Maximum: 20% (s.26(3) and (5)).
- Proportionate reduction for partial service (s.27).
- New establishments: bonus only in profit years during the first 5 accounting years (s.26(6)).
- Set-on/set-off of allocable surplus up to the 4th year (s.36); worked example in Wages Rules Appendix A.
- Gross profit computation: Wages Rules Appendices B (banks), C (others) and D (further deductions), rules 24–26.

**Payment:** "by crediting it in the bank account ... within a period of eight months from the close of the accounting year" (s.39(1)), so **by 30 November** for an April–March year. The appropriate Government may extend this, up to 2 years in total.

**Adjustments:**
- Disqualification on dismissal for fraud, riotous or violent behaviour, theft/misappropriation/sabotage, or conviction for sexual harassment (s.29).
- Puja/customary or interim bonus can be adjusted (s.37).
- Financial loss from misconduct in that year can be deducted (s.38).

**Contract labour:** if the contractor fails to pay, the principal employer pays the minimum bonus on written notice (Wages Rules r.21; KPMG).

**Registers and forms:**
- Old Payment of Bonus Rules 1975 Forms A (allocable surplus), B (set-on/set-off), C (bonus paid to employees) and D (annual return) are **superseded** (Wages Rules supersession item (ix), confirmed-official). The Form A–D descriptions are from prior knowledge, not re-verified.
- **No replacement register is prescribed.** Annual reporting is via unified return Part I section H: number covered, total bonus paid, date paid.

**FY 2025-26** (secondary): bonus for the year straddling the PoB Act and the Code is due by **30 Nov 2026**.

## 6. Maternity benefit (Code on Social Security, Chapter VI, ss.59–73)

Statute and rules are confirmed-official unless marked otherwise.

**Entitlement:**
- 26 weeks, of which no more than 8 before the expected delivery date; 12 weeks (no more than 6 before) for a woman with 2 or more surviving children (s.60(3)).
- 12 weeks for adopting a child under 3 months, or for a commissioning mother, from handover (s.60(4)).
- Eligibility: at least 80 days actually worked in the 12 months before the expected delivery (s.60(2)).
- Paid at the average daily wage of the 3 calendar months before absence, subject to the minimum wage (s.60(1)).
- Work from home after the benefit period, by agreement (s.60(5)).
- No employment for 6 weeks after delivery, miscarriage or MTP (s.59).

**Medical bonus:** ₹3,500 or the notified amount, if no free pre- and post-natal care is provided (s.64). It is paid with the maternity benefit (r.35(2)(d)).

**Nursing breaks:** two breaks of 15 minutes each, plus up to 15 minutes' travel time (r.36).

**Crèche:**
- Mandatory where 50 or more employees are ordinarily employed; within 1 km; for children under 6 (r.37).
- By agreement, a crèche allowance of at least ₹500 per child per month can replace the facility, for up to 2 children. The agreement is with the negotiating union or council, or with the majority of employees if neither exists. The allowance covers women, widowers and single parents.

**Process and forms:**
- Certificate of pregnancy, delivery etc.: Form X.
- Notice of claim, nomination and receipt of payment: Form XI. A plain-paper or electronic notice may be accepted. The nominee's Aadhaar is required.
- Pre-delivery benefit is paid in advance on proof; the balance within 48 hours of proof of delivery (s.62(5)).
- Wages for leave under s.65 are paid within 48 hours of the Form X certificate (r.35(2)(f)).
- On the woman's death, payment within 2 months (r.35(2)(e)).
- Appeal: Form XII. Complaint to the Inspector-cum-Facilitator: Form XIII-A; appeal against that: Form XIII-B.
- The employer must give these forms free on request (r.40(2)).

**Register:** Register of Women Employees, Form XXII (columns in §2.2 G).

**Display:** abstract in Form XIV (r.40(4)).

**Returns:**
- No separate maternity return under the central rules.
- Reported in unified annual return Part I section G: female employees; how many availed maternity leave; how many were paid medical bonus; wage deductions, if any.
- Companies also include a Board's Report compliance statement (secondary).

**Applicability:** SS Code First Schedule, Chapter VI: every factory, mine and plantation, and shops/establishments with 10 or more employees (secondary; schedule text not fetched). ESIC-covered women draw maternity benefit from ESIC under Chapter IV (s.61).

## 7. Gratuity (Code on Social Security, Chapter V, ss.53–58)

**Payable** (s.53(1)) on termination after 5 years' continuous service: superannuation; retirement or resignation; death or disablement; **expiry of fixed-term employment**; or a notified event.
- Five years is not required for death, disablement or expiry of fixed-term employment (2nd proviso).
- Working journalists: 3 years.

**Fixed-term employees:**
- s.53(2) 3rd proviso: gratuity "on pro rata basis".
- SS Rules r.33(1)(a) proviso: "an employee on fixed term employment shall be eligible for gratuity, if he renders service under the contract for a period of at least one year". A further period of 6 months or more but under 1 year is rounded up to one more year.
- MoLE FAQ: fixed-term employees are "eligible for Gratuity on completion of one year of service" (secondary report).
- **Product rule:** fixed-term employees are eligible at 1 year or more of service; amount is pro rata; months above 6 round up.

**Amount** (s.53(2) and Explanation 3):
- 15 days' wages per completed year, or part year over 6 months, at the last-drawn wage rate.
- Monthly-rated: monthly wage ÷ 26 × 15.
- Seasonal: 7 days per season.
- Piece-rated: average of the last 3 months, excluding overtime.

**Continuous service** (s.54): deemed for 240 days worked in 12 months (190 for underground work or a under-6-day week), or 120/95 days for 6 months. Maternity leave up to 26 weeks counts.

**Ceiling** (s.53(3), "as notified"): **₹20 lakh** per a Government letter of 3 Aug 2026, as reported (secondary). No new S.O. under the Code was found; the last was S.O. 1420(E) of 2018 under the 1972 Act. Re-notification under the Code is **NOT CONFIRMED**.

**Wage base:** the Code "wages" with the 50% add-back (§4), applicable from 21 Nov 2025 (MoLE FAQ, secondary).

**Forfeiture:** s.53(6).

**Process (SS Rules r.32–33):**
- Nomination in Form III, in duplicate, within 90 days of completing 1 year. The employer verifies it and returns an attested duplicate within 30 days. Fresh nomination within 90 days of acquiring a family. The nominee's Aadhaar is required.
- Application in Form IV within 30 days of gratuity becoming payable (legal heirs: 1 year).
- Within 15 days the employer issues Form V. If the claim is admissible, Form V fixes a payment date no later than the 30th day after the application; if not, it gives reasons, copied to the competent authority.
- Payment by demand draft or bank credit.
- Disputes: Form VI (direction), Form VII (notice/summons), Form VIII (payment notice), Form IX (recovery).

**Registers and returns:** no gratuity register or separate return is prescribed. The Payment of Gratuity (Central) Rules 1972 and their forms are superseded (confirmed-official). The SS unified annual return (Form XXIII) applies to employers covered by Chapter V and VI, but it has no gratuity-specific section.

**Applicability:** establishments with 10 or more employees (First Schedule; secondary).
