# Professional Tax on salaried employees: primary-source verification

Verified 2026-10-26/27. Scope: 18 levying jurisdictions (MH, KA, TN, TG, AP, GJ, MP, AS, BR, JH, MN, ML, MZ, NL, SK, TR, PY, PB), plus 15 where no levy is expected (CG, GA, DL, HR, UP, RJ, HP, UK, JK, LA, CH, AN, DH, AR, LD). West Bengal, Kerala and Odisha were out of scope; they are verified separately.

Leads came from `docs/compliance-research/2026-10-26-state-obligations.md`. Product values were compared with `database/data/compliance/in.php` (lines 159–177) and `app/Domain/Compliance/Services/StatutoryEngine.php` (lines 80–105).

## Method and labels

**Labels.** Each jurisdiction has exactly one label:
- `OFFICIAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED`: the slabs and the employer frequency and due date were read in an official text. Any remaining minor gaps are marked NOT CONFIRMED field by field.
- `OFFICIAL_SOURCE_PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`: some core facts are official; others are secondary or NOT CONFIRMED.
- `SECONDARY_SOURCE_ONLY`
- `CONFLICTING_SOURCES`
- `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `UNVERIFIED`

**Primary sources used:**
- state tax or finance department websites and portals
- state gazettes, or gazette PDFs hosted on department sites
- prsindia.org copies of gazetted Acts
- official budget receipt documents (used for the absence checks)

**OCR.** Scanned PDFs were read with tesseract and are marked "(OCR)". A single digit could be misread in those quotes.

**TLS.** `curl -k` was never used. Where a site served an incomplete certificate chain, the issuing intermediate was fetched from the certificate's AIA URL and added to a CA bundle. Sites with real failures were not bypassed:
- jharkhandcomtax.gov.in: certificate hostname mismatch
- biharcommercialtax.gov.in and state-tax.bihar.gov.in: no connection
- comtax.cg.nic.in: certificate expired

A few north-eastern sites could be read only over plain HTTP; they are flagged in their sections.

**Delegated passes.** The six north-eastern states and the 15 absence checks were run as two delegated verification passes. Their full sections, with verbatim excerpts, are appended at the end of this file (Parts B and C). I spot-checked the Mizoram slab table against the official 2013 notification (OCR); its band structure matches.

**Search.** The web-search budget ran out partway through. Later facts were verified only by fetching official pages directly.

Gross basis: the engine applies the slab to `$c->gross()` for the month. Several Acts define "salary" differently:
- MH and SK exclude bonus and gratuity.
- MN excludes HRA, conveyance and other compensatory allowances.
- MP, BR and TR refer to "salary" as defined in s.17 of the Income-tax Act.
- MP, BR, MN and JH set their bands on *annual* salary.
- TN and PY set theirs on *half-yearly* income.

## Part A: Levying states verified in this pass

### MH: Maharashtra (OFFICIAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED)

**Law.** Maharashtra State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1975 (Mah. XVI of 1975), and the Rules of 1975. The official consolidated Act is dated "[Text as on 16th July 2025]".

**Slabs.** Schedule I entry 1 was substituted by Mah. Act No. XIX of 2023, published 6 Apr 2023, which "shall come into force on the 1st April 2023":
> "(i) in case of men, whose monthly salaries or wages,–– (a) do not exceed rupees seven thousand five hundred; Nil. (b) exceed rupees seven thousand five hundred but do not exceed rupees ten thousand; one hundred seventy-five per month. (c) exceed rupees ten thousand; two thousand five hundred per annum to be paid in following manner :–– (a) two hundred per month except for the month of February; (b) three hundred for the month of February; (ii) in case of women, whose monthly salaries or wages,–– (a) do not exceed rupees twenty-five thousand; Nil. (b) exceed rupees twenty-five thousand; two thousand five hundred per annum … (a) two hundred per month except for the month of February; (b) three hundred for the month of February."

The department's rate schedule "As on 31.03.2025" shows the same entry under "1/4/2023 onwards".

**Salary basis.** s.2(h):
> "'salary' or 'wage' includes pay or wages, dearness allowance and all other remunerations received by any person on regular basis … and also includes perquisites, and profits in lieu of salary, as defined in section 17 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 … but does not include bonus in any form and on any account or gratuity"

**Exemptions.** s.27A covers:
- (a) armed forces serving in the State
- (b) "the badli workers in the textile industry"
- (c) persons with benchmark disability, and parents or guardians of a child with benchmark disability, holding a certificate (substituted from 1-Apr-2023)
- (d) women agents under the Mahila Pradhan Kshetriya Bachat Yojana
- (f) "the person who have completed the age of sixty-five years"
- (h) armed members of the CRPF and BSF serving in the State

**Registration.** s.5 "Registration and Enrolment": the employer obtains a certificate of registration (PTRC); other persons obtain a certificate of enrolment.

**Payment and return.**
- Rule 11(1): pay "on or before the date prescribed for filling of such return".
- Rule 11(2A): e-return in **Form III-B**; payment by challan **MTR-6**, "made before uploading the said return".
- Frequency, under Rule 11(3) as amended on 6-Jun-2019 (No. PFT-2019/C.R.8/Taxation-3): "'was less than Rs. 1,00,000'" → **annual** return; "'was Rs. 1,00,000 or more'" → **monthly** return, based on previous-year liability.
- Rule 11A: in the year registration is granted, file monthly returns.

**2026 change.** Due dates were advanced by the Amendment Rules 2026: Finance Dept notification No. व्यकर-2026/C.R.4/Taxation-3, 28 Feb 2026 (Gazette Extraordinary Part IV-B No. 91):
> "in clause (a), for the figures and words '31st of March' the figures and words '15th of March' shall be substituted ; (2) in clause (c), for the words 'last date' the figures and words '15th day' shall be substituted"

The illustration now reads 15 May and 15 June. In effect:
- **Monthly:** the return and payment are due by the 15th of the month in which salary is paid. April salary paid in May is due by 15 May.
- **Annual:** due by **15 March** of the year to which it relates, covering the previous March plus April–February.

**Source hosting.** The full Rule 11(3) consolidated text was read on a secondary host (comply360). The 2019 and 2026 amending notifications were read on mahagst.gov.in.

### KA: Karnataka (OFFICIAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED)

**Law.** Karnataka Tax on Profession, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1976 (Karnataka Act 35 of 1976), and the Rules of 1976. The department hosts a consolidated copy of the Rules "as on 31-03-2019".

**Slabs.** Schedule entry 1 was substituted by Karnataka Act No. 14 of 2023 (Gazette 14-03-2023, "with effect from the 1st day of April, 2023"):
> "Salary or wage earners whose salary or wage or both, as the case may be, for a month is Rs. 25,000-00 and above — Rs. 200-00 per month"

Column 3 was then substituted by Karnataka Act No. 33 of 2025 (Notification DPAL 08 SHASANA 2025, Gazette 15-04-2025; "It shall come into force with effect from the first day of April, 2025"):
> "(i) Rs.200-00 per month (except for the month of February); (ii) Rs.300-00 for the month of February"

Two statements on the official employer portal agree:
> "PT for Feb-2026 to be deducted at Rs. 300.00 per employee instead of Rs. 200.00 as per Notification No:DPAL 08 SHASANA 2025"

> FAQ: "Salaried individuals with a monthly gross income of less than Rs. 25,000/- per month with effect from 01/04/2023" are exempt.

**Exemptions (portal FAQ).** "Blind, deaf and dumb individuals or people diagnosed with nearly 40% of permanent disabilities, Employee having single child with sterilization certificate from district surgeon, Combatant and civilian-non-combatant members of Armed Forces etc". A Simpliance listing of a 60+ exemption could not be confirmed for salaried employees. The 01-04-2026 Commissioner notification exempts *enrolled* senior citizens (60+) from furnishing returns only.

**Monthly statement and payment.** s.6-A(1), quoted in the Removal of Difficulties Orders 01/2020 and 01/2023:
> "every employer registered under this Act to furnish, within twenty days of expiry of the month, a statement showing therein the salary and wages paid by him and the amount of tax deducted"

Rule 11-A: the statement is in **Form 5-A**. The FAQ says an employer with "EVEN ONE employee" must "make the payment within 20th of succeeding month".

**Annual return.** **Form 5** (rule 11(1): "The return under sub-section (1) of Section 6 shall be furnished in Form-5"). Its due date ("within 60 days" of the year end, about 30 May) is from secondary sources; the s.6(1) text was NOT CONFIRMED.

**2025–26 changes.**
- February ₹300 from FY 2025-26.
- Rule 27 (service of notices) substituted by the Amendment Rules 2025 (Gazette 19-09-2025).
- Karnataka Act 22 of 2026, w.e.f. 1-Apr-2026: enrolled persons who have paid are deemed to have filed.
- RoD Order 01/2026: enrolled persons' FY 2026-27 payment due 5 May 2026.
- Portal upgrade from 1-Jul-2026 (press release 08-07-2026).

None of these changes employer slabs.

### TN: Tamil Nadu (OFFICIAL_SOURCE_PARTIALLY_VERIFIED)

**Law and collector.** PT is levied by local bodies. For Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) the levy is under s.117-B(2) of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998 (amended 2022); employer deduction is under s.117-C; procedure is in Rules 277–278 of the TN Urban Local Bodies Rules, 2023.

**GCC press release R.D. PT C.No/3555/2026, 04.08.2026:**
> "under Section 117-C of the Tamil Nadu Urban Local Bodies Act, 1998, every employer is legally responsible for deducting Profession Tax from the salary or wages of employees and remitting the same"

> "As per Rule 277(2) … Profession Tax shall be deducted by the employer … from the salary payable through the respective pay bills for the months of August and January every year."

> "under Rule 277(3), the Profession tax deducted from employees in the month of August 2026 from salary have to remit to Greater Chennai Corporation on or before 30 September 2026, together with the prescribed return for revocery in Form-14."

The return listing each employee is "FORM - 15 [See rule 278 (1) (a) and (5)]", which carries "Half-Yearly Gross Income" for each employee.

**Slabs.** From the GCC Revenue Department page and the press release:
> "Half-Yearly gross Income … Half yearly Profession Tax w.e.f. 01.04.2018 / Half yearly Profession Tax w.e.f. II/2024-2025: Upto 21,000 NIL NIL; 21,001–30,000 135/- 180/-; 30,001–45,000 315/- 425/-; 45,001–60,000 690/- 930/-; 60,001–75,000 1025/- 1025/-; 75,001 Above 1250/- 1250/-"

**Not confirmed:**
- The remittance date for the January deduction (Rule 277(3) text not read; likely by 31 March).
- Rates and dates for municipalities, town panchayats and village panchayats outside GCC.

## Method and limits (read first)

  - Did a 0028-107 line exist?
  - Where the summary table lists components, do they add up to the stated total? If they do, nothing is hidden in another line.

  For UTs without a legislature, I used the **Union Receipt Budget 2026-27**, item 10 "Taxes of Union Territories".
