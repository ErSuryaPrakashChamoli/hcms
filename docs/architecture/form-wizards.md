# Record forms as wizards

Every record form is filled in step by step, the way the policy wizard (`App\Filament\Pages\PolicyWizard`) works: one section per step, Previous / Next, and a last **Review** step that lists what was filled in (create) or changed, before → after (edit), with the reason for change and the Create / Save button. Introduced 2026-10-29 at the owner's request ("a wizard to follow for all, not one long form").

## How it works

- **`App\Filament\Support\FormWizard`** turns a form's top-level components into steps: each titled `Section` (or `Fieldset`) is a step; consecutive loose fields become one step named after the record ("Shift", "Attendance device"); `Hidden` fields and the reason for change (`AuditReasonField`) go to Review. The steps reuse the form's own components, so fields, validation, relationships and the save path are unchanged. A step is shown while something in it is visible.
- **`App\Filament\Support\Forms\PeopleWizard`** is Filament's wizard with one change: Next always validates the step being left, also when steps can be opened freely.
- **Record pages** (`PeopleCreateRecord`, `PeopleEditRecord` via `Pages\Concerns\FollowsFormWizard`): always a wizard. Creating goes in order (earlier steps can be reopened); editing can open any step from the step list. The page's submit and cancel buttons move to the wizard's last step. A page that builds its own wizard (Filament's `HasWizard`, e.g. the hire wizard `CreateEmployee`) keeps it, with the same layout. A page can opt out by overriding `usesFormWizard()`.
- **Drawers** (`PeopleCreateAction` / `PeopleEditAction`, bound in place of Filament's `CreateAction` / `EditAction` by `PeopleOsUi::register()`, via `Actions\Concerns\FollowsFormWizard`) and **action modals** (`PeopleAction` / `PeopleBulkAction`, bound in place of `Action` / `BulkAction`): a wizard when `FormWizard::wants()` — two titled sections or more (`DRAWER_MINIMUM`), or four fields or more (`MODAL_MINIMUM_FIELDS`). A short note, status change or quick edit stays one screen (drawers keep the review panel underneath). Action modals use mode `action` ("…then confirm"); read-only modals and the small modals of a form's own controls (a select's "create option", rich editor blocks, repeaters: `getSchemaComponent()` set) never become wizards. An action given its own `->steps()` keeps them, in the same `pos-wizard pos-wizard-drawer` style. Rule set by the streamline programme (2026-11-05) and narrowed by its review (2026-11-10: "do not force a wizard onto every small form").
- **Long runs of fields** are split into steps of at most `FormWizard::LOOSE_STEP_MAXIMUM` (6), in the form's order: the first named after the record ("Details" in action modals), then "More details", "More details (2)".
- **Custom pages** use the same engine: `SecurityPolicyPage`, `SetupCentre` (each module's settings; ungrouped settings in parts of six), `PayCyclePage` and `ExpenseFormSetup` build their sections into the schema, then wrap them with `FormWizard::steps($schema, FormWizard::EDIT, …)` and a submit action on Review. `DeclareInvestments` and the hire wizard build their own steps with the same page rail; the hire wizard ends in the same Review (`FormWizard::review()`). The guided payroll run (`RunPayroll`) and the policy wizard keep a step rail drawn the same way.
- **Review** (`resources/views/filament/shell/wizard-review.blade.php`, `FormWizard::summary()`) uses the browser-side `posFormReview` component (`resources/js/peopleos.js`) in its `'all'` scope: it reads every step of the wizard and lists **every detail the person went through** (2026-11-19, owner: "the review must show every detail"), grouped by step and by titled section inside a step, with what was left empty shown as *Not filled in*. Editing lists every detail too and marks what changed (*Changed*, before → after, tinted), with a *Show only changes* switch. It reads each kind of field as the person sees it: selects (single and multiple) by their option names, radios, checkbox lists and toggle buttons by the chosen options' names (not their descriptions), toggles and checkboxes as Yes/No, date pickers as shown, text with its prefix/suffix (₹, %, days), rich text and Markdown as written, uploads by file name, tags, key-value pairs, and repeaters/builders/table repeaters one line per item ("1. Label: value · Label: value"). Secrets are masked; fields hidden in the browser are left out. It treats what the form shows before the person first changes a field as the starting point (selects and date pickers that finish loading late are not changes); its baseline resets only after a save (`save` / `create` calls), not after Next. The short panel under a one-screen drawer form (`form-review.blade.php`) keeps the `'changes'` scope: only what was filled in or changed.
- **Wizards that build their own steps** end in the same full list (`FormWizard::endInReview()`, applied to every action and drawer with its own `->steps()` by `Actions\Concerns\FollowsFormWizard`): when the last step is already a review (named Review, Confirm, Check, Summary, Preview or Submit) the list of every detail is put above what it shows (who approves it, the first number, a confirmation); otherwise a Review step is added after the last step. The investment declaration (`DeclareInvestments`) puts the same list above its totals; the policy wizard's own review already lists every answer by step; the hire wizard ends in `FormWizard::review()`.
- **What confirming does** (2026-11-26, owner: "beautify and arrange it to make it more readable … across the entire HCM"): a Review step that works out an outcome shows it under the full list as one **outcome card**, `App\Filament\Support\Review\Outcome` (view `resources/views/filament/shell/outcome.blade.php`, styles `theme.css` section J2), never as run-on sentences or "Label: value" lines. The card has a header (what it is — "What happens when you apply" —, the outcome in one sentence, the status said once as a pill: *Ready to …*, *Read before …*, *Cannot be … yet*), then the body in the order the caller builds it (problems as a toned list, before → after as two panels of chips with joining in green and leaving struck through, facts as ruled label / value rows like the list above, figures as stat tiles, the people reached as a short list with "and N more", per-employee lines as a table) and the small print as one-line notes, each with an icon for what it says (*Checked:*, *Kept:*, *Refused:*, *How it runs:*, *Next:*). It decides nothing: callers pass what their services worked out, and a status pill appears only where the code knows readiness. It is `Htmlable`, so `Text::make(fn (Get $get) => …)` returns it directly; Blade views build it in a `@php` block. Used by Applies to…, Company values…, Clone, the bulk time-office and workforce actions, payroll, tax and compensation wizards, and on-behalf requests. The full list above it also names fields whose label is hidden (by their section, placeholder or step) and lists the labelled details a step shows without asking; the banner's line says once that nothing is saved until the person confirms and that it is then recorded in the audit trail.
- **After saving** (2026-11-19, owner: "once saved or submitted, redirect to the listing page of that section"): record pages (`PeopleCreateRecord`, `PeopleEditRecord`, via `Pages\Concerns\ReturnsToListing`) go back to the resource's list, when the person may open it, and the *Created* / *Saved* notice has **Open** for the record (its view page, else its edit page) — records whose setup goes on inside them (a calendar's holidays, a survey's questions) are one click away. No resource page overrides `getRedirectUrl()` except the hire wizard, which first shows what the hire created and what is left, with *Go to the employee list* (guard test in `FormWizardTest`). Drawers and action wizards close and stay on the list they were opened from. Settings pages without a list (`SetupCentre`, `PayCyclePage`, `ExpenseFormSetup`, `SecurityPolicyPage`, via `Pages\Concerns\StartsOverAfterSave`) open again from their start once saved. A submitted investment declaration goes to Payroll → Tax declarations (else My investment proofs); a saved draft stays open. The policy wizard already returns to the policy list.
- **Layout** (`theme.css`, section J): on record pages the steps sit in a sticky rail beside the step (from 1024 px); in drawers they wrap above the step and the buttons stay in view; on phones every step is a numbered dot with the current one named.

## Conventions for new forms

- Put fields in titled `Section`s, 2–6 fields each, named the way an HR administrator would say it. Put the reason for change at the top level (`AuditReasonField::make()->visibleOn('edit')`); the wizard moves it to Review.
- A drawer form with 8 or more fields must have at least two sections. `tests/Feature/Experience/FormWizardTest.php` ("keeps long drawer forms in sections") fails otherwise.
- Never wrap fields into a section that has `->relationship()` or `->statePath()` just to make a step: that changes where the data is saved.

## Not converted

- Action modals with fewer than four fields (a reason, a date, one choice): a wizard would only add a click.
- Auth and signup screens (sign-in, password change, invitation, the public signup pages): short, outside the panel shell.
- Hand-written one-line inputs (comments, discussions, the assistant's chat box) and filter bars.
