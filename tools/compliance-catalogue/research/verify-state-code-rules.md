# State and UT rules under the four Labour Codes: verification matrix (as of 26/27-10-2026)

Scope: 36 states and UTs × 4 Codes:
- Code on Wages 2019 (**CoW**)
- Industrial Relations Code 2020 (**IR**)
- Code on Social Security 2020 (**SS**)
- OSH & Working Conditions Code 2020 (**OSH**)

Baseline:
- The Codes have been in force since 21-11-2025.
- Central rules: G.S.R. 342(E) IR, 343(E) Wages, 344(E) SS and 345(E) OSH, all of 08-05-2026.
- PIB 21-11-2025: until rules under the Codes are made, the old Acts' rules continue.

Method:
- Six parallel research passes, one per state group, read the state gazettes or labour-department PDFs. Where the official site was unreachable, they read gazette prints hosted on Simpliance, SGCMS or LKS.
- I spot-checked Bihar CoW r.43 and Rajasthan CoW r.42 against the downloaded gazette text myself.
- Detailed evidence, with quotes and every URL, is in the group files in `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-administrator-Documents-hrms-hcms/51009900-f0f6-4a55-b43b-4a523f8012c3/scratchpad/state-rules/`:
  - `group-A1-GJ-RJ-AP-AR.md`
  - `group-A2-BR-UP-UK.md`
  - `group-B-UTs.md`
  - `group-C-MH-KA-TN-TG-KL-HR-PB-MP.md`
  - `group-D-AS-CG-GA-HP-JH-OD-WB-SK.md`
  - `group-E-MN-ML-MZ-NL-TR.md`
- Downloaded PDFs are in the `dl_*` folders next to those files.

**Limits:**
- The shared web-search budget (200 searches per turn) ran out in the late stages. Later checks used direct fetches of official sites only.
- These official sites were unreachable or behind a captcha: Mizoram e-gazette, UP gazette, Chhattisgarh/Jharkhand/West Bengal gazettes, HP Rajpatra, Tripura e-gazette, Chandigarh labour site, MP labour site and the A&N gazette index.
- A final notified after a department listing was last updated may therefore be missed. Gaps are marked NOT CONFIRMED.

## Label legend

Every state × Code finding carries exactly one label. The matrix uses the abbreviation; the per-state notes use the full label.

| Abbreviation | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **OPV** | OFFICIAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED | The gazette or notification text itself was read, from an official site or a gazette print. For a DRAFT cell, an official listing was also checked and shows no later final. |
| **OSPV** | OFFICIAL_SOURCE_PARTIALLY_VERIFIED | An official source was seen but is incomplete. Examples: a signed order but not the gazette issue; a listing or title only; a draft read but the search for a later final could not be completed. |
| **SSO** | SECONDARY_SOURCE_ONLY | Only secondary sources were available. |
| **CS** | CONFLICTING_SOURCES | Sources disagree. |
| **LRR** | LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED | Status cannot be settled from the texts alone. |
| **UNV** | UNVERIFIED | Not checked. |

Status notation:
- **FINAL** dd-mm-yyyy is the gazette date. The effective date is given where it differs.
- **DRAFT** dd-mm-yyyy is the latest draft found.
- **NONE** means no draft or final was found.

  - UP (all four)

  - BR (all four)
  - GJ CoW and OSH preambles
- **No final rule expressly lets old-format registers or returns continue.** The only carry-over provision found is AP OSH r.175(2): existing registrations and licences remain valid if particulars are furnished within 6 months.
- **Code-level savings:**
  - IR s.104(2), SS s.164(2)(a) and OSH s.143(3) expressly save rules under the repealed Acts until replaced.
  - CoW s.69(2) saves notifications, appointments and orders but does not say "rule"; continuation of old wage rules rests on GCA s.24 (LRR).
- **Pre-commencement rules:**
  - GJ (2021/2023/2025) and AR (2021/2022) tie commencement to the Code's commencement, so they took effect 21-11-2025.
  - Neither was re-notified to align with the central rules of 08-05-2026. GJ SS was amended 13-02-2026; AR 2026 amendments are NOT CONFIRMED.
  - SK CoW 2022 says it commences on final publication (28-04-2022), before the Code commenced (LRR).
  - MZ IR/SS 2023 commence on a date to be appointed; none was found (LRR).

## 6. Union Territories: who is the appropriate government?

LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED overall. Nothing in the texts states expressly that the central rules do or do not apply to private establishments in UTs.

**Code definitions:**
- **CoW:** s.2(d)(ii) makes the "State Government" the appropriate government for other establishments. **s.2(w): "'State' includes a Union territory."**
- **SS:** s.2(3)(b) makes the State Government the appropriate government. **s.2(80):** "State Government" includes (a) for a UT with a legislature, the Government of the UT; (b) for a UT without a legislature, the Administrator under Art. 239.
  - Note s.2(3)(a): for an establishment with **branches in more than one State**, the **Central Government** is the appropriate government.
- **IR** s.2(b) and **OSH** s.2(1)(d) have no UT definition, so the General Clauses Act applies. GCA s.3(60)(c) is quoted from the brief; the text was not re-fetched.

**MHA delegations (OPV, Gazette of India):**
- **S.O. 26(E)** (IR) and **S.O. 27(E)** (OSH), both 02-01-2026, entrust the LG/Administrator of all 8 UTs with the powers of the "appropriate Government or State Government".
  - Delhi, A&N, DNH&DD, Chandigarh, Puducherry, Lakshadweep, Ladakh and J&K.
  - URLs: https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/269090.pdf and https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/269091.pdf
- No equivalent CoW or SS delegation was found. It may not be needed, given s.2(w) and s.2(80).

**Central rules:**
- Wages and SS central rules have no UT clause.
- OSH r.1(2) "extend to the whole of India" is territorial extent only.
- IR (Central) r.3(1) reads references to the Central Government as the Administrator "in relation to an industrial dispute in respect of a Union territory without legislature … for which the appropriate Government is the Central Government". This is ambiguous.

**Conclusion (LRR):**
- For private shops, offices and factories in every UT, the **UT administration or government** is the appropriate government. The central rules (G.S.R. 342–345(E)) therefore do **not** directly govern them.
- **Exceptions:**
  - central-sphere establishments
  - SS matters of establishments with branches in more than one State
- All UTs except LA, LD and (for IR/OSH) JK have only drafts, so the old UT rules continue.

**Delhi, J&K and Puducherry (UTs with legislatures):**
- Each has its own drafts.
- J&K is FINAL for IR and OSH.
- The split of powers between the LG and the elected government was NOT VERIFIED; the GNCTD Act, JKRA and GUT Act texts were not fetched.

## 8. Legal-review items (LRR) and NOT CONFIRMED points

1. **Annual-return due date conflicts.**
   - In GJ, AR, JK and MZ the OSH rule says the unified return is due 1 Feb, while the form's instructions say 28/29 Feb.
   - BR and UP need both the OSH return and an SS return with different dates: BR 28/29 Feb and 1 Feb; UP 31 Jan and 1 Feb.
2. **Wage-slip timing conflicts.** The RJ and BR OSH rules say "before one day of payment". Their CoW rules say "on or before payment".
3. **UP CoW Form XIII muster roll** is appended but not cited by any rule. **UK CoW** has no muster form, and its annual-return cross-reference points to OSH rules that are still draft.
4. **LD CoW r.45** annual-return cross-reference is circular.
5. **Pre-commencement rules.**
   - SK CoW 2022 commenced on publication in 2022.
   - MZ IR/SS 2023 have no appointed date.
   - GJ and AR rules were not re-aligned with the 2026 central rules; AR amendments are NOT CONFIRMED.
6. **Commencement anomalies.** LA OSH r.1(c) is retroactive on its face, and the LA SS effective date is 03-08 or 10-08-2026. The LA and LD instruments cite superseded MHA delegations.
7. **Meghalaya.** The status of the 21-11-2025 "DRAFT RULES" printings is unresolved (all four Codes).
8. **Gazette dates not confirmed:** JK IR and OSH, UP IR and MZ CoW.
