## 1. Confidence model

| Label | Meaning | Counts as verified? |
|---|---|---|
| OFFICIAL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_VERIFIED | The instrument's own text was read on a government host (`*.gov.in`, `*.nic.in`) over HTTPS, or as a gazette PDF whose Government of India Press signature validates | Yes, if no legal review is open |
| OFFICIAL_SOURCE_PARTIALLY_VERIFIED | Official text seen only in part: a gazette print on a non-government host (PRS, Simpliance, SGCMS, LKS, StaffNews, TaxHeal), an official FAQ, press note, rate page or listing rather than the instrument, a draft read when the final was not, or a government page reached only over unencrypted HTTP | No: pending verification |
| SECONDARY_SOURCE_ONLY | Commentary, vendor guides, payroll-software pages, **cited** | No: legally unverified |
| CONFLICTING_SOURCES | Sources disagree, and the conflict is stated | No |
| LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED | The text is known, but applying it needs a legal judgement | No |
| UNVERIFIED | Not checked, could not be checked, or nothing cited | No |

Rules applied throughout:
- A secondary source never overrides an official one and is never upgraded.
- A catalogue entry is "legal source verified" only when it is primary-verified and has no open legal review.
- Absence of a levy is labelled by the evidence for absence (a budget with no head, a department's list of the laws
  it enforces). Local-body levies were not checked.
- Facts not found are written "NOT CONFIRMED". They are never filled in.
- Dates are kept apart: publication, notification, commencement, effective date and filing deadline.

## 2. Limits of this verification

- **TLS was never bypassed** (no `-k`, `--insecure` or HTTP downgrade).
  - Where a government server sent an incomplete certificate chain (dpal.karnataka, egazetteharyana, egazette,
    pblabour, mptax, commercialtax.gujarat, psdt.punjab), the missing intermediate was fetched from the
    certificate's own issuer URL (AIA), as browsers do. It was verified against the system root store, in a copy of
    the store; the system store was not changed.
  - Hosts with expired or mismatched certificates were recorded as failures and not read: Manipur VAT,
    comtax.cg.nic.in, cglabour.gov.in, www.labour.wb.gov.in, the Karnataka Legislature.
- **Servers that refuse modern TLS clients:** comtax.wb.gov.in (legacy renegotiation; its PDFs were read through a
  separate fetch client), the Punjab Assembly, and sikkimtax.gov.in (legacy TLS only).
- **egazette.gov.in** sends only its own certificate. Central gazette texts were read from labour.gov.in copies or
  signed PDFs instead.
- **Unreachable or captcha-protected:** the Haryana e-Gazette search (CAPTCHA); India Code (HTTP 504 throughout the
  second pass); the Bihar tax hosts (DNS); labour sites of BR, JH, JK, NL, MN, AN and LA; finance.arunachal.gov.in.
- Searches were budgeted. After the budget ran out, only known official URLs were fetched. A notification published
  after a department's last listing could have been missed; such points are marked NOT CONFIRMED.
- Scanned documents were read by OCR. A single digit can be misread in those quotes. The minimum-wage tables must be
  checked by eye before they are loaded as rule data.
- Nothing here is legal advice. Professional review is an owner action (D6).
