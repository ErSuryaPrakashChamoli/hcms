**This is not a claim of India-wide coverage.**
- Only 12 of 36 states and UTs have final rules under at least one Labour Code. Elsewhere the earlier Acts' rules and registers continue.
- Shops-and-establishments facts were re-checked for 24 states and UTs in the second pass: six now rest on primary sources (MH, TN, GJ, HR, PY, TG's integrated register), and the rest on partial or secondary ones (`india-compliance-source-register.md` §9).
- PeopleOS computes PT and LWF in payroll only where a rule version exists (PT: MH, KA, TG, AP, WB, TN, GJ, MP, KL; LWF: MH, KA, TG, DL, TN, GJ, HR).
